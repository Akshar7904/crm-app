Getting Started & Login

The Enterprize360 HR & Payroll Management System is a comprehensive, role-based web platform covering the complete employee lifecycle — from onboarding through payroll, leave, attendance, expense management, and asset tracking. All data is live and role-controlled.

Step 1

Open your browser and navigate to the system URL provided by your IT administrator. The system works best on Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge (Chromium), or Firefox. Internet Explorer is not supported.

Step 2

Enter your credentials. Type your registered email address and password, then click Login. First-time users receive their credentials by email when their account is created.

Step 3

You will be taken to the Dashboard — the main home screen. The left sidebar contains all navigation links. The sidebar shows only the items your role allows you to see.

Step 4

First-time setup checklist for administrators:

  • Add all Departments (Organization → Departments)
  • Add all Designations / Job Titles (Organization → Designations)
  • Add public holidays for the current year (Attendance → Holidays)
  • Set up the Chart of Accounts (Expenses → Chart of Accounts) — SYSADMIN only
  • Add Vendors (Expenses → Vendors)
  • Set Opening Balance for the financial year (Expenses → Opening Balance)
  • Add Employees (Employees → Add Employee)
Sidebar navigation: Click any top-level item to expand its sub-menu. Click a sub-item to navigate directly to that page. The active page is highlighted in the sidebar.
Forgot your password? Click Forgot Password on the login page. A reset link will be sent to your registered email address. The link expires after 24 hours.

User Roles & Permissions

Every user account is assigned exactly one role. The role controls which pages, buttons, and data records the user can access. Only ADMIN and SYSADMIN users can change another user's role.

Employee ROLE_USER

  • View and download own payslips
  • Submit and track leave requests
  • Submit expense claims
  • Clock in and out (via Dashboard or WhatsApp)
  • View own attendance records
  • Upload and download own documents
  • View company announcements
  • Request and track company assets
  • Log school attendance (interns only)

Manager ROLE_MANAGER

  • All ROLE_USER permissions
  • View all employees and their leave requests
  • Approve or reject leave requests
  • View all attendance records
  • View department expense claims
  • View team employee documents
  • View all announcements (manage view)
  • View all assets and asset requests
  • View intern school attendance requests
  • View Expenses module (read-only)

Admin ROLE_ADMIN

  • All ROLE_MANAGER permissions
  • Add, edit, deactivate employees
  • Manage departments & designations
  • Process payroll runs & generate payslips
  • Approve/reject all leave & expense claims
  • Create and send invoices to clients
  • Manage clients and vendors
  • Record and approve business expenses
  • Manage assets and assignments
  • Post and delete announcements
  • Manage public holidays
  • Set Opening Balance & Annual Budget
  • Approve/reject intern school attendance
  • Change user roles (cannot promote to SYSADMIN)

System Admin ROLE_SYSADMIN

  • All ROLE_ADMIN permissions
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Assign any role including ROLE_SYSADMIN
  • Configure the Chart of Accounts
  • Access full P&L financial reports
  • System-wide configuration access
  • No employee record required (can still use leave)
Principle of least privilege: Only assign the role that matches what the user actually needs. Avoid giving all staff ROLE_ADMIN — this reduces the risk of accidental data changes and unauthorised access. To change a user's role, navigate to Employees → All Employees, open the employee profile, and use the Change Role control at the top of the profile page.

Departments & Designations

Set up your organisational structure before adding employees. Departments group employees by business unit (e.g. Finance, IT, HR). Designations define job titles (e.g. Software Developer, Accountant).

Add a Department

Navigate to Organization → Departments. Click Add Department. Enter the department name and an optional description. Click Save. The department immediately appears in employee profile dropdowns.

Edit / Delete a Department

Click the Edit icon on the department row to rename it. A department can only be deleted if no employees are currently assigned to it. Renaming a department immediately updates all linked employee profiles.

Add a Designation

Navigate to Organization → Designations. Click Add Designation. Enter the job title and the department it belongs to. Click Save. Designations appear on payslips.

Best Practice

Create all departments and designations before adding employees. This prevents orphaned records and ensures reports are organised correctly from day one.

Employee Management

Navigate to Employees → All Employees to view, add, and manage all staff records. Route: /employee

Step 1 — Add a New Employee

Click Add Employee or navigate to /employee/new. The form has the following sections:

  • Personal Information: First name, last name, email, phone, ID number, date of birth, gender, nationality, home address
  • Job Information: Department, designation, employment type (Full-time / Part-time / Contract / Intern), start date, salary
  • Banking Details: Bank name, account number, account type (Cheque/Savings/Transmission), branch code — used for payroll EFT payments
  • Emergency Contact: Contact name, relationship, and phone number

Click Create Employee. A system user account is automatically created and login credentials are emailed to the employee.

Step 2 — View & Edit an Employee

Click any employee row to open their full profile. All fields are editable by ADMIN/SYSADMIN. Make changes and click Save Changes. Banking detail changes take effect on the next payroll run.

Step 3 — Change a User's Role

Open the employee profile. At the top of the page, find the Role field. Select the new role from the dropdown and click Update Role. Only ADMIN and SYSADMIN can perform this action. SYSADMIN is required to grant or revoke the SYSADMIN role.

Step 4 — Search & Filter

Use the Search bar to find employees by name, email, department, or designation. Use the filter dropdowns to narrow by employment type or status (Active / Inactive).

Step 5 — Employee Documents

On the employee profile, navigate to the Documents tab. Click Upload Document to add files (e.g. signed contract, SARS tax certificate). Select document type, add a description, choose the file, and click Upload. Admins can verify documents by clicking Verify, marking them as confirmed.

Step 6 — Upload or Change Profile Photo

On the employee profile page, click the profile photo or the camera icon to upload a new photo on behalf of the employee. Supported formats: JPG, PNG (max 5 MB). ADMIN and SYSADMIN can update any employee's photo from the employee profile page.

Step 7 — Reset Employee Password (Admin)

Open the employee profile and navigate to the Security tab (visible to ADMIN and SYSADMIN only — not shown on your own profile). Enter the new password in the New Password field, confirm it in Confirm Password, and click Reset Password. This does not require the employee's current password. Use this when an employee is locked out or has forgotten their credentials. Notify the employee of their new password through a secure channel.

Step 8 — Deactivate an Employee

When an employee leaves, set their status to Inactive on their profile — do not delete. This preserves all payroll, leave, and document history. The system login is automatically disabled when the account is deactivated.

Banking accuracy: Verify banking details match the employee's bank statement exactly. Incorrect account numbers cause failed EFT payments, which can take several business days to reverse and may incur bank charges.

Leave Management (Admin)

Navigate to Leave Management → All Leave Applications (route: /admin/leave) to manage all employee leave requests. Tabs: Pending, Approved, Rejected, Upcoming, All, Allocate.

Step 1 — View Pending Requests

The Pending tab (default) shows all leave requests awaiting your decision. Each row shows: Employee name, Leave type, Date range, Number of days, Submission date, and Status badge. Use the Search bar or the Leave Type dropdown to filter the list.

Step 2 — Approve a Leave Request

Click the green Approve button (tick icon) on the pending request row. A confirmation modal appears. Optionally add Remarks. Click Confirm Approve. The employee is notified. The leave balance is debited automatically.

Step 3 — Reject a Leave Request

Click the red Reject button (cross icon). A modal opens requiring a Rejection Reason (mandatory). Enter a clear reason, then click Confirm Reject. The employee is notified with your rejection reason. The leave balance is not affected.

Step 4 — View Upcoming Leave

The Upcoming tab shows all approved leave in the next 30 days. Use this for workforce planning — identify coverage gaps before approving more leave in the same period.

Step 5 — Leave Allocation (Balance Management)

Click the Allocate tab. Use the Hire Year filter to show employees who joined in a specific year. The table shows each employee's leave balances by type:

  • Total Entitled: The number of days allocated for the year (editable)
  • Carried Over: Days carried over from the previous year
  • Used: Days already taken (approved leaves)
  • Pending: Days in PENDING requests not yet approved
  • Available: = Total Entitled + Carried Over − Used − Pending

To adjust an entitlement manually, edit the Total Entitled field inline and click Save on that row. Changes take effect immediately.

Leave types available: Annual Leave, Sick Leave, Family Responsibility Leave, Maternity Leave, Parental Leave, Adoption Leave, Study Leave, Unpaid Leave, Special Leave. Each type accrues and tracks independently.
Doctor's notes: If an employee marks "Doctor's Note Attached" on their sick leave request, a download link appears in the admin view. Click it to download and verify the document before approving.

Leave Balance Explained

Understanding how leave balances are calculated is essential for accurate workforce planning and payroll reconciliation.

Balance Formula

Available = Total Entitled + Carried Over − Used − Pending

  • Total Entitled: The number of leave days allocated for this year (set by admin or auto-accrued)
  • Carried Over: Unused days from the previous year that were rolled over
  • Used: Days deducted for approved and taken leave
  • Pending: Days reserved by leave requests that have not yet been approved or rejected
Monthly Accrual Model (Annual Leave)

Annual Leave builds gradually throughout the year at 1.25 days per month (= 15 days per year). Leave does NOT get credited upfront at the start of the year. An employee who joins in March will have accrued 1.25 × 10 = 12.5 days by December. This aligns with the Basic Conditions of Employment Act (BCEA).

Sick Leave

Sick Leave is governed by a 3-year cycle. Under the BCEA, an employee is entitled to 30 days over 36 months (for a 5-day work week). In this system, the entitlement is set manually per employee. A doctor's note is required for absences of more than 2 consecutive days.

Carry-Over Rules

At the start of each new leave year, unused Annual Leave days are carried over to the next year (subject to company policy). The system admin can adjust the carried-over amount in the Allocate tab. Family Responsibility, Study, and Maternity leave do not carry over.

Hire-Year Filter (Allocate Tab)

The Hire Year filter on the Allocate tab shows employees whose employment start date falls within the selected year. This helps you manage leave allocations for cohorts — e.g. all employees hired in 2024 may be on a different entitlement cycle than 2023 hires.

Leave calculations only count business days (Monday–Friday, excluding public holidays). A request from Monday to Friday = 5 days. A request that spans a public holiday = n−1 days.

Attendance Management

Weekly Attendance Grid

Attendance Reports

Use the date-range filter to generate attendance summaries. The report shows total days present, late arrivals, and absences per employee for the selected period. Approved leave days are excluded from absence counts automatically.

My Attendance (All Users)

All employees can view their own attendance at Attendance → My Attendance (route: /attendance/my). This shows a personal history of clock-in/out times, hours worked, and status for each day.

Holiday Management

Navigate to Attendance → Holidays (route: /holidays). Holidays are excluded from leave-day calculations and attendance absence counts.

Add a Holiday

Click New Holiday. Enter: Name (e.g. "Human Rights Day"), Date, and Type (Public Holiday or Company Holiday). Click Save. The holiday is immediately reflected in all leave and attendance calculations.

Edit or Delete

Click the Edit icon to change the name or date. Click Delete to remove a holiday. Deleting a holiday does not retroactively change already-approved leave records.

Annual Maintenance

At the start of each year, review and add the new year's South African public holidays. The BCEA gazette is published annually by the Department of Employment and Labour. Failure to add holidays results in incorrect leave day counts for requests that span those dates.

South African statutory public holidays include: New Year's Day, Human Rights Day, Good Friday, Family Day, Freedom Day, Workers' Day, Youth Day, National Women's Day, Heritage Day, Day of Reconciliation, Christmas Day, Day of Goodwill — plus any election days gazetted.

Payroll Processing

Navigate to Payroll. The payroll module covers salary management, payroll runs, payslip generation, and payroll statistics. Only ADMIN and SYSADMIN can process payroll.

Step 1 — Set Employee Salaries

Navigate to Payroll → Employee Salary (/payroll/salaries). Each employee has a base salary, allowances, and deductions configured here. These values are used automatically when processing a payroll run.

Step 2 — Process a Payroll Run

Navigate to Payroll → Process Payroll (/payroll/process) or click Process Payroll on the Dashboard. Select the Month and Year for the pay period. Review the employee list and click Run Payroll. The system calculates gross salary, deductions (PAYE, UIF, SDL), and net pay for each employee.

Step 3 — Review the Payroll Run

Navigate to Payroll → All Payrolls (/payroll/list). Click on a payroll run to open its detail page (/payroll/detail/:id). Review earnings, deductions, and net pay totals before sending payslips.

Step 4 — View & Send Payslips

Navigate to Payroll → Payslips (/payroll/payslips). Select the month/year to view all generated payslips. Employees can be sent their payslips via email directly from this view. Each payslip shows: Employee name & ID, Pay period, Earnings breakdown (basic + allowances), Deductions breakdown (PAYE, UIF, SDL), and Net Pay.

Step 5 — Payroll Statistics

Navigate to Payroll → Statistics (/payroll/stats) for visual analytics: monthly payroll cost trend, department cost breakdown, headcount changes, and average salary by designation.

Do not process the same month twice without first voiding or adjusting the existing payroll run. Processing a second run for the same period will create duplicate payslips. Contact your system administrator if a run needs to be reversed.

Expenses Module

The Expenses module is a QuickBooks-style business expense management system. Navigate to Expenses in the sidebar. Available to MANAGER, ADMIN, and SYSADMIN roles.

Overview Dashboard

Navigate to Expenses → Overview (/expenses/overview). Shows a summary of total income, total expenses, and net profit/loss for the selected month/year. Includes a trend chart and expense category donut chart. Use the month/year selector at the top to change the period.

Record a Business Expense

Navigate to Expenses → Record Expense (/expenses/entry) — available to ADMIN and SYSADMIN only. Fill in: Expense Date, Vendor, Account (Chart of Accounts category), Amount, Description, Payment Method, and attach a receipt if available. Click Save Expense. The expense appears in the All Expenses list and feeds the P&L report.

View All Business Expenses

Navigate to Expenses → All Expenses (/expenses/entry/list). Filter by date range, vendor, account, or payment method. Edit or delete any expense record. Exports are available for accounting reconciliation.

Financial Reports (P&L)

Navigate to Expenses → Reports (/expenses/reports). Generate an Income Statement (Profit & Loss) for a selected year and month range. Click Export to Excel to download a formatted spreadsheet. The P&L is calculated as: Total Revenue (Invoices) − Total Business Expenses − Payroll Cost = Net Profit.

Chart of Accounts

Navigate to Expenses → Chart of Accounts (/expenses/accounts) — SYSADMIN only. Add, edit, or deactivate account categories (e.g. "Office Supplies", "Travel", "Marketing"). Every business expense must be classified under an account category. Good categorisation produces accurate P&L reports.

Vendors

Navigate to Expenses → Vendors (/expenses/vendors). Add suppliers and service providers. Each vendor record includes: name, contact person, email, phone, address, and tax number. Vendors are linked to business expense entries.

Opening Balance & Annual Budget

Navigate to Expenses → Opening Balance (/expenses/opening-balance) to set the starting account balance for the financial year. Navigate to Expenses → Annual Budget (/expenses/budget) to enter budget targets per expense category. The Budget vs Actual view (/expenses/budget-vs-actual) compares planned vs real spend for the year.

Expense Claims (Admin)

Expense Claims are employee reimbursement requests (e.g. petrol, meals, travel). Navigate to Claims → All Claims (/expense-claims). Distinct from business expenses recorded by admins.

Review Pending Claims

The claims list defaults to showing all claims. Filter by status using the tab bar: PENDING APPROVED REJECTED. Each row shows: Employee, Category, Amount, Date, and Status.

Approve a Claim

Click the Approve button on a pending claim. Optionally add an approval note. Click Confirm. The employee is notified and the claim moves to Approved status. Approved claim amounts are included in the Expenses P&L as an employee expense line.

Reject a Claim

Click Reject. Enter a clear rejection reason (required). Click Confirm Reject. The employee is notified with your rejection reason. Rejected claims are not included in any financial calculations.

PENDING

The claim has been submitted by the employee and is awaiting admin review. The amount is reserved but not yet counted as approved expenditure.

APPROVED

The claim has been approved. The amount is included in expense reporting. The employee should receive reimbursement in the next payroll or petty cash run.

REJECTED

The claim was declined. No financial impact. The rejection reason is visible to the employee in their My Claims view.

System Administration

System administration tasks are reserved for ADMIN and SYSADMIN roles. Key administrative operations are performed within the standard module pages — there is no separate "Settings" page.

Changing a User's Role

Navigate to Employees → All Employees, open the employee profile, and find the Role section at the top. Select the new role from the dropdown and click Update Role. Only SYSADMIN can grant or revoke the SYSADMIN role. Regular ADMIN can change roles between ROLE_USER, ROLE_MANAGER, and ROLE_ADMIN.

Theme

The system operates in light mode only. Dark mode is not available in this version.

User Account Creation

User accounts are created automatically when you add a new employee. There is no separate "create user" screen. The user account is tied to the employee's email address. If a user needs access without an employee record (e.g. a SYSADMIN account), the account must be created directly in the backend by a developer or database administrator.

Session & Security

Sessions use JWT tokens stored in browser local storage. Sessions expire after a period of inactivity. If a user's account needs to be disabled immediately (e.g. an employee who leaves), set their employee status to Inactive on their profile. They will no longer be able to authenticate.

Admin Password Reset

ADMIN and SYSADMIN users can reset any employee's password without requiring the employee's current password. Open the employee profile, click the Security tab, enter a new password (minimum 8 characters), confirm it, and click Reset Password. A confirmation message is shown on success. Communicate the new password to the employee securely — the system does not send an automatic email for admin-initiated resets.

New User Registration Notifications

When a new user registers or an employee account is created, an email notification is sent to all SYSADMIN users in the system. If there are no SYSADMIN accounts, it falls back to notifying all ADMIN users. This ensures that account creation is always visible to system administrators.

Notifications

System notifications (leave approvals, payslip generation, etc.) appear in the Messages section of the sidebar (/notifications). The notification bell badge count updates in real time. Click any notification to navigate to the relevant record.

Frequently Asked Questions

An employee doesn't see the Intern menu even though they are an intern. Why?

Check the employee's Employment Type in their profile. It must be set to INTERN (exact value). The sidebar uses this field to conditionally show the Intern section. Also verify their role is not explicitly excluded.

I ran payroll but the payslips are showing wrong amounts. What do I do?

Do NOT run payroll again for the same month. First, verify the employee salary records (Payroll → Employee Salary). If the base salary or deductions were incorrect, edit them, then contact your SYSADMIN to void and re-run the affected month's payroll from the backend.

An employee's leave balance shows negative available days. Is this correct?

A negative balance can occur if the employee has been approved for more days than their entitlement allows, or if their entitlement has not yet accrued to cover their approved leave (monthly accrual). Go to the Allocate tab, find the employee, and adjust their Total Entitled to reflect the actual approved amount, or consult your HR policy on negative leave balances.

The P&L report shows no income. Why?

Income in the P&L comes from Invoices marked as Paid. If no invoices have been marked as paid for the selected period, income will show as zero. Check the Invoices module and ensure paid invoices are correctly updated.

How do I give a SYSADMIN account access to leave features if they have no employee record?

The system attempts to find an employee record linked to the SYSADMIN's user account (by user ID). If none exists, the leave page shows a "No employee record" message. To give a SYSADMIN leave access, create an employee record for them in the Employees section and link it to their user account — or ask a backend developer to create the link directly in the database.

Important: Your department and designation must be set in your employee profile before you can submit a leave request. If you see an error about your profile being incomplete, contact your HR administrator to update your department and designation.

Understanding Your Leave Balance

How Available Days are Calculated

Available = Total Entitled + Carried Over − Used − Pending

  • Total Entitled: Your total leave allocation for this year (set by HR)
  • Carried Over: Unused days rolled over from last year
  • Used: Days already taken on approved leave
  • Pending: Days in requests you have submitted but that have not yet been approved
Annual Leave Accrual

Annual Leave is not given all at once at the start of the year. It builds up monthly at 1.25 days per month (= 15 days per year for a full year). If you joined in March, you will have earned 1.25 × 10 = 12.5 days by December. This is standard under the Basic Conditions of Employment Act.

Why Can't I Apply?

If you see an error when trying to apply for leave, the most common reasons are: (1) Your employee profile is incomplete (missing department or designation — contact HR); (2) You don't have enough available days of that type; (3) Your leave request dates are invalid (end date before start date).

Leave calculations only count working days (Monday–Friday). Weekends and public holidays within your leave period are not deducted from your balance.

Frequently Asked Questions

I can't submit a leave request — it says my profile is incomplete.

Your employee profile is missing a Department or Designation. Contact your HR administrator and ask them to update your profile. Once your department and designation are set, you can submit leave requests.

My leave balance shows 0 but I haven't taken any leave this year.

Annual Leave accrues at 1.25 days per month. If it is early in the year (e.g. February), you may only have 2.5 days accrued so far. Contact HR if you believe the balance is incorrect, as they can adjust your entitlement in the Leave Allocation screen.

I forgot to clock in this morning. What do I do?

You cannot correct your own attendance records. Contact your line manager or HR administrator. They can edit your attendance record via the admin attendance view to add your correct clock-in time.

My payslip shows the wrong salary amount.

Contact your HR administrator immediately. Payslip corrections are made by processing a payroll adjustment in the next pay cycle. Do not try to edit the payslip yourself.

I don't see the Intern section in the sidebar even though I'm an intern.

Your Employment Type in your employee profile must be set to INTERN. Contact HR and ask them to update your employment type. Once updated, the Intern menu will appear in your sidebar on your next login (or page refresh).

My expense claim was rejected. Can I resubmit it?

Yes. Review the rejection reason shown on the rejected claim. Correct the issue (e.g. attach a valid receipt, fix the amount) and submit a new claim. You cannot edit a rejected claim — you must create a new one.

I can't log into the system.

First, try the Forgot Password link to reset your password. If you still can't log in, your account may have been deactivated (e.g. after your employment was ended). Contact your HR administrator or IT department.
