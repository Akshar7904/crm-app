System Overview

The LKCentrix HR Payroll Management System is a comprehensive, web-based platform designed to centralise all human resource and financial operations for your organisation. It covers the full employee lifecycle from onboarding to payroll, leave management, expense control, client invoicing, and asset tracking.

The system is role-based — every screen, button, and data record is controlled by the user's assigned role. Administrators have full visibility and control; employees see only what is relevant to them.

Browser requirement: The system works best in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge (Chromium), or Mozilla Firefox. Internet Explorer is not supported. A stable internet connection is required.
2

User Roles & Permissions

Roles are assigned when creating a user account and can be changed at any time by a System Administrator. Each role inherits the permissions of all roles below it.

Employee ROLE_USER

  • View own payslips & download PDF
  • Submit and track leave requests
  • Submit expense claims
  • View own attendance records
  • Upload and download own documents
  • View company announcements
  • Request and track company assets
  • Log school attendance (interns only)

Manager ROLE_MANAGER

  • All Employee permissions
  • View team leave and attendance
  • Approve or reject leave requests
  • View department expense claims
  • View team employee documents

Admin ROLE_ADMIN

  • All Manager permissions
  • Add, edit, delete employees
  • Manage departments & designations
  • Process payroll runs & send payslips
  • Approve or reject all leave & claims
  • Create and send invoices
  • Manage clients and vendors
  • Record and approve business expenses
  • Manage assets and asset assignments
  • Post and manage announcements
  • Manage public holidays

System Admin ROLE_SYSADMIN

  • All Admin permissions
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Assign and change user roles
  • Configure Chart of Accounts
  • Access full P&L financial reports
  • Set opening balances & budgets
  • System-wide configuration
Principle of least privilege: Only assign the role that matches what the user actually needs. Avoid giving all staff ROLE_ADMIN — this reduces the risk of accidental data changes and unauthorised access.

Organization — Departments & Designations

Before adding employees, set up your organisational structure. Departments group employees by business unit (e.g. Finance, IT, HR). Designations define job titles (e.g. Software Developer, Accountant, HR Manager).

Manage Departments

Navigate to Organization → Departments. Click Add Department and enter the department name and an optional description. Click Save. Departments appear in the employee profile's department dropdown. To rename a department, click the Edit icon on its row. Deleting a department is only possible if no employees are currently assigned to it.

Manage Designations

Navigate to Organization → Designations. Click Add Designation and enter the job title, department it belongs to, and an optional salary grade or description. Designations link to employees and appear on payslips and employment documents.

Best Practice

Create all departments and designations before adding employees. This prevents orphaned records and ensures payslips and reports are organised correctly from the start. If you rename a department or designation after employees have been assigned, the change reflects immediately on all linked employee profiles.

Add a New Employee

Click New Employee. The form is divided into sections:

  • Personal Information: First name, last name, email, phone number, ID number, date of birth, gender, and home address
  • Job Information: Department, designation, employment type (Full-time / Part-time / Contract / Intern), start date, and salary
  • Banking Details: Bank name, account number, account type, and branch code — used for payroll payments
  • Emergency Contact: Name, relationship, and phone number of a person to contact in an emergency

Once all required fields are completed, click Create Employee. A system user account is automatically created and credentials are sent to the employee's email address.

Edit an Employee

Click on any employee in the list to open their profile. All fields are editable. Scroll down to find the relevant section, make your changes, and click Save Changes. Changes to banking details take effect on the next payroll run.

Search & Filter Employees

Use the search bar at the top of the employee list to find an employee by name, email, department, or designation. Use the filter dropdowns to narrow results by employment type or status (Active / Inactive).

Employee Documents (Admin)

Open any employee profile and navigate to the Documents tab. Click Upload Document to add files on behalf of the employee (e.g. signed contract, SARS tax certificate). Select the document type, add an optional description, choose the file, and click Upload. All documents are securely stored and downloadable at any time. You can also verify documents by clicking the Verify button, which marks the document as confirmed by an admin.

Deactivate an Employee

When an employee leaves the organisation, change their status to Inactive on their profile rather than deleting them. This preserves all historical payroll, leave, and document records. Their system login will no longer work once the account is deactivated.

Data accuracy: Ensure banking details are captured exactly as they appear on the employee's bank statement. Incorrect account numbers will result in failed payroll payments, which can take several business days to reverse.

Attendance Management

The Attendance module tracks employee presence records. Navigate to Attendance → All Attendance for the admin view.

View Attendance Records

The attendance list shows all employee check-in and check-out records. Filter by date range, department, or employee to find specific records. Each row shows the employee name, date, check-in time, check-out time, total hours worked, and status (Present / Late / Absent).

Edit an Attendance Record

If a check-in or check-out time was captured incorrectly (e.g. due to a system or clock issue), click the Edit icon on the record. Adjust the time and save. All edits are logged with the admin's name and timestamp for audit purposes.

Attendance Reports

Use the date range filter to generate an attendance report for a given period. The report shows total days present, total late arrivals, and total absences per employee. This data can inform performance reviews and leave reconciliation.

Holidays & Leave Exclusions

Public holidays (managed under Holiday Management) are automatically excluded from attendance calculations. Approved leave days are also excluded from absence counts — the system reconciles attendance with approved leave to give accurate absence figures.

Holiday Management

Navigate to Attendance → Holidays to manage the list of public and company holidays. Holidays affect leave calculations and attendance records.

Add a Holiday

Click New Holiday. Enter the holiday name (e.g. "Human Rights Day"), the date, and whether it is a Public Holiday (statutory) or Company Holiday (organisation-specific day off). Click Save. The holiday is immediately reflected in leave and attendance calculations.

Edit or Delete a Holiday

Click the Edit icon on any holiday to change its name or date. Click Delete to remove a holiday. Deleting a holiday does not retroactively affect approved leave — it only affects future calculations.

Annual Setup

At the start of each year, review and add the new year's public holidays (South African public holidays are gazetted annually). Failure to add holidays can result in incorrect leave day counts if employees submit leave that spans a public holiday.

South African public holidays are fixed by the Public Holidays Act. You are responsible for keeping this list up to date annually. Refer to the Department of Employment and Labour's gazette for the official dates.

Leave Management — pending requests with one-click approve / reject actions

Review Pending Requests

The admin leave page defaults to showing all Pending requests at the top. Each row shows the employee name, leave type, start and end dates, number of working days, and the reason provided. Click on any row to see the full request detail.

Approve a Leave Request

Click the Approve button (green tick) on a pending request. You may optionally add a comment before confirming. Once approved, the employee's leave balance is immediately reduced by the number of approved days and the employee receives a system notification.

Reject a Leave Request

Click the Reject button (red X). A dialog will prompt you for a rejection reason — this is mandatory and is displayed to the employee in their notification. Provide a clear, professional reason (e.g. "Insufficient staffing during the requested period. Please choose alternative dates.").

Log Leave on Behalf of an Employee

Click Log Leave on the admin leave page. Select the employee from the dropdown, choose the leave type, enter the start and end dates, and provide a reason. This is used for situations such as retrospective sick leave after a doctor's note is received, or when an employee cannot access the system themselves.

Filter & Search

Use the status tabs (All / Pending / Approved / Rejected) and the search field to filter leave records by employee name, leave type, or date. The summary cards at the top show counts for each status at a glance.

Leave Balance Overview

Each approved leave request automatically deducts from the employee's leave balance. To check an employee's remaining balance, open their Employee Profile and navigate to the Leave tab — this shows remaining days per leave type for the current cycle.

Legal note: Under the Basic Conditions of Employment Act (BCEA), employees are entitled to a minimum of 15 working days annual leave per year. Sick leave is 30 days over a 3-year cycle. Ensure leave balances are configured correctly and reviewed regularly.

Payroll — monthly summary with gross, deductions, net salary, and payslip status per employee

Generate Payroll

Click Generate Payroll. Select the pay period (month and year) and choose whether to run payroll for all employees or a specific individual. The system automatically calculates:

  • Gross Salary — basic salary plus any allowances (transport, housing, etc.)
  • PAYE (Pay As You Earn) — income tax deducted based on the applicable SARS tax table for the year
  • UIF (Unemployment Insurance Fund) — 1% of gross salary (up to the UIF ceiling) deducted from the employee and matched by the employer
  • Pension / Provident Fund — where applicable, deducted per the company policy percentage
  • Net Salary — gross salary minus all deductions

Review the payroll summary before confirming. Click Confirm & Generate to create payslips for all selected employees.

View & Manage Payslips

Navigate to Payroll → Payslips. The payslip list shows all generated payslips by period. Use the period selector to load payslips for a specific month. Click on any payslip to view the full breakdown. Payslips are read-only once generated — if a correction is needed, contact your system administrator to void and regenerate.

Download a Payslip

Open any payslip and click Download PDF. The downloaded PDF is a formatted, print-ready document suitable for submission to banks, SARS, or other institutions as proof of income. The PDF includes the company letterhead, employee details, earnings breakdown, deductions, and net pay.

Send Payslip via Email

On the payslips list, click the Send (envelope) icon next to any payslip. The system will email the payslip as a PDF attachment directly to the employee's registered email address. A confirmation toast notification will confirm that the email was sent. You can also send payslips in bulk by selecting multiple employees and clicking Send Selected.

Payroll History

All generated payroll runs are permanently stored and accessible from the payslips page. Filter by year or period to retrieve historical payroll data. This is useful for audits, employee verification letters, and annual tax reconciliations.

SARS compliance: PAYE and UIF deductions must be submitted to SARS by the 7th of the following month. Ensure payroll is processed and figures are reconciled before the submission deadline. The system calculates deductions but submission to SARS via eFiling or your payroll bureau is a separate process.

Expense Claims Management

Navigate to Expenses → Claims to review and action all employee expense reimbursement requests.

Review Pending Claims

The claims table defaults to all claims with status filters at the top (All / Pending / Approved / Paid / Rejected). Each row shows the claim number, employee name, submission date, purpose, total amount, and current status. Click on any claim to view the full detail including line items and attached receipts.

Approve a Claim

Open the claim and click Approve. Optionally add an approval comment. The claim status changes to Approved and the employee is notified. Approved claims are included in the P&L under Employee Reimbursements.

Reject a Claim

Click Reject and provide a reason — for example, "Receipt not attached" or "Expense does not fall within company policy". The employee will see this reason in their notification and can resubmit a corrected claim if appropriate.

Mark as Paid

Once an approved claim has been reimbursed via EFT or payroll, click Mark as Paid to update the status to Paid. This triggers a notification to the employee confirming their reimbursement. Keeping payment status up to date is important for accurate P&L reporting.

Export Claims Report

Click Export on the claims page to download an Excel report of all claims for the selected period. The export includes all line items, amounts, statuses, and employee details — useful for month-end reconciliation and audit documentation.

Business Expenses & Chart of Accounts

This module handles direct company expenses (rent, utilities, subscriptions, travel) that are not employee reimbursements. It follows a QuickBooks-style double-entry model where every expense is posted against a Chart of Account.

First-time Setup — Seed Chart of Accounts

Navigate to Expenses → Chart of Accounts. If the account list is empty, click Seed Default Accounts. This creates 13 standard accounts:

  • 4000 — Service Revenue (Income)
  • 4100 — Other Income (Income)
  • 5000 — Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
  • 6000 — Payroll & Salaries (Operating Expense) — linked to Payroll module
  • 6100 — Rent & Office (Operating Expense)
  • 6200 — Utilities (Operating Expense)
  • 6300 — Travel & Transport (Operating Expense)
  • 6400 — Meals & Entertainment (Operating Expense)
  • 6500 — Marketing & Advertising (Operating Expense)
  • 6600 — Software & Subscriptions (Operating Expense)
  • 6700 — Office Supplies (Operating Expense)
  • 6800 — Employee Reimbursements (Operating Expense) — linked to Claims
  • 6900 — Other Expenses (Operating Expense)
Add a Custom Account

Click Add Account to create a new chart of account. Assign an account code (e.g. 6950), a descriptive name, account type (Income / COGS / Operating Expense / Other), and set it as Active. Custom accounts appear in the expense entry form and P&L report.

Manage Vendors

Navigate to Expenses → Vendors. Click Add Vendor and enter the vendor name, category (Supplier / Contractor / Utility / etc.), contact email, phone, address, and VAT number. Vendors appear in the expense entry form's vendor dropdown. Keeping vendors up to date ensures accurate reporting by supplier.

Record a Business Expense

Navigate to Expenses → Record Expense. Complete the form:

  • Expense Date — the date the expense was incurred
  • Vendor — select from the vendor list, or leave blank if no vendor applies
  • Account — select the Chart of Account to post against (e.g. Rent & Office)
  • Description — brief description (e.g. "March office rent — 4th floor")
  • Amount (excl. VAT) — the net amount
  • VAT Amount — the VAT portion (15% for standard-rated expenses)
  • Total Amount — auto-calculated: Amount + VAT
  • Payment Method — Cash / Bank Transfer / Credit Card / Cheque
  • Receipt — attach a receipt or invoice (PDF, JPG, PNG)

Click Save as Draft to save without approving, or Save & Approve to post it immediately to the P&L.

View All Expenses

Navigate to Expenses → All Expenses to see every recorded business expense. Filter by date range, account, vendor, or status (Draft / Approved / Void). Click on any expense to view or edit it. Click Void to cancel an approved expense if it was recorded in error — voided expenses are retained for audit purposes but excluded from the P&L.

Financial Reports & P&L

Navigate to Expenses → Reports for the full financial reporting suite, including the Profit & Loss (Income Statement).

Overview Dashboard

The Expenses Overview (Expenses → Overview) shows KPI cards for: Total Income, Gross Profit, Total Expenses, and Net Profit/Loss for the selected period. The net profit card is green when positive and red when the organisation is operating at a loss. A 12-month trend chart shows income vs. expenses side by side, and a donut chart breaks down expenses by account category.

Profit & Loss Statement

The P&L report aggregates data from three sources:

  • Income: total of all invoices in the period (excluding cancelled)
  • COGS: direct cost expenses tagged as Cost of Goods Sold
  • Operating Expenses: payroll costs + employee claims + direct business expenses, grouped by account

Formula: Gross Profit = Income − COGS  |  Net Profit = Gross Profit − Total Operating Expenses

Export Income Statement to Excel

Click Download Income Statement (.xlsx). The exported file contains 4 sheets:

  • Sheet 1 — Income Statement: full P&L with all line items, totals, and Net Profit/Loss (green or red font)
  • Sheet 2 — Business Expenses Detail: every recorded expense with date, vendor, account, amount, VAT, and status
  • Sheet 3 — Invoice Summary: all invoices with customer, date, amounts, and status
  • Sheet 4 — Payroll Summary: all payroll records with gross salary, deductions, and net salary per employee

Select the year and month range before downloading to scope the report to your required period.

Invoice & Client Management

Navigate to Clients and Invoices in the sidebar to manage all client billing.

Add a Client

Navigate to Clients → New Client. Fill in: full name, email address, phone number, client type (Individual / Institution / Corporate), status (Active / Pending / Inactive / Banned), profile image URL (optional), and address. Click Create Client. The client is now available in the invoice form's client dropdown.

Edit a Client Profile

Click on any client in the client list to open their profile. All fields are editable. The profile page also shows the client's full invoice history with total amounts and statuses. Click Save Changes after editing.

Create an Invoice

Navigate to Invoices → New Invoice. Complete the invoice form:

  • Client — select from the client dropdown
  • Invoice Date — the date the invoice is issued
  • Due Date — the payment deadline
  • Line Items — add one or more services/products with description, quantity, unit price, and VAT rate
  • Subtotal, VAT & Total — auto-calculated from line items
  • Notes — optional payment terms or additional information shown on the invoice

Click Create Invoice. The invoice is saved with status Pending.

Send an Invoice

Open any invoice and click Send Invoice. A confirmation popup appears showing the client's name and email address. Click Yes, Send Invoice to confirm. The system emails the invoice as a formatted PDF to the client. A notification confirms successful delivery. Important: verify the client's email address is correct before sending — once sent, the email cannot be recalled.

Update Invoice Status

Invoice statuses: Pending (issued, awaiting payment), Paid (payment received), Overdue (past due date), Canceled (invoice voided). Open the invoice and click Edit to update the status manually when payment is received. Paid invoices are included in the P&L income calculation.

Asset Management

Navigate to Assets in the sidebar to manage all company-owned equipment and resources.

Add a New Asset

Navigate to Assets → All Assets and click Add Asset. Enter the asset details:

  • Asset Name — descriptive name (e.g. "Dell Latitude 5540 Laptop")
  • Asset Code / Serial Number — unique identifier for physical tracking
  • Category — e.g. IT Equipment, Office Furniture, Vehicle, Tools
  • Purchase Date and Purchase Price — for depreciation tracking
  • Condition — New / Good / Fair / Poor
  • Location — physical location (e.g. "Head Office — Floor 2")
  • Notes — any additional information (warranty expiry, vendor, etc.)

Click Save Asset. The asset is now available for assignment.

Assign an Asset to an Employee

Navigate to Assets → Assign Asset or open the asset detail page and click Assign. Select the employee from the dropdown, set the assignment date, and add any handover notes (e.g. condition at time of handover). Click Confirm Assignment. The asset status changes to Assigned and both the asset record and employee profile reflect the assignment.

View All Assignments

Navigate to Assets → Assignments to see every current and historical assignment. Filter by employee, asset category, or date range. Each row shows the asset name, assigned employee, assignment date, and return date (if returned).

Process Asset Return

When an employee returns an asset, open the assignment record and click Mark as Returned. Record the return date and any notes about the asset's condition on return. The asset status reverts to Available and can be reassigned to another employee.

Manage Asset Requests

Employees can request assets through the system (see Employee Guide — Asset Requests). Navigate to Assets → Requests to view all pending requests. Click Approve to fulfil the request (which triggers an assignment) or Reject with a reason. Employees are notified of the outcome.

Announcements

Navigate to Announcements in the sidebar to post and manage company-wide communications.

Create an Announcement

Click New Announcement. Fill in:

  • Title — a clear, concise heading (e.g. "Office Closure — 16 June 2025")
  • Content — the full announcement text. Use line breaks for readability
  • Priority — Normal or Urgent. Urgent announcements are highlighted prominently on the employee dashboard
  • Target Audience — All employees, or a specific department

Click Publish. The announcement is immediately visible to all targeted employees on their dashboard and the Announcements page. Employees also receive a notification alert.

Edit or Delete an Announcement

Click the Edit icon on any announcement to update its content. Click Delete to remove it permanently. Deleting an announcement does not remove it from employees' notification history — only from the main announcements list.

Best Practice

Use announcements for important, time-sensitive company communications. For routine reminders, prefer email or team messaging tools. Keep announcement content concise and professional — remember it is visible to all employees.

User Account Management

User accounts are separate from employee records, but linked. An employee record holds HR data; a user account holds login credentials and role. Only System Administrators can manage user accounts.

View User Accounts

Navigate to your profile menu (top-right) and select User Management, or access it from the admin settings. The user list shows all registered accounts, their email addresses, assigned roles, and account status (Active / Locked).

Change a User's Role

Click on a user account and select a new role from the Role dropdown. Click Save. The change takes effect on the user's next login. Inform the user of their new role so they know which features are now available to them.

Lock / Unlock an Account

If an employee has left or their account should be temporarily suspended, click Lock Account. A locked account cannot log in but all data is preserved. Click Unlock to restore access. This is the preferred approach over deletion, which permanently removes the account.

Reset a User's Password

If an employee cannot reset their own password (e.g. they no longer have access to their email), an admin can trigger a password reset from the user management page. Click Reset Password and the user will receive an email with a reset link.

When an employee is terminated, lock their user account immediately on their last day or before. Do not wait for the next payroll cycle. Delayed account deactivation is a significant security risk.

Opening Balance & Budget

These modules are available to System Administrators and are used for financial period setup and budgetary planning.

Opening Balance

Navigate to Finances → Opening Balance. When migrating from a previous accounting system or starting a new financial year, enter the opening balance for each Chart of Account category. This ensures the P&L report reflects accurate carry-over figures rather than starting from zero. Enter the opening amount per account and the effective date, then click Save Opening Balance.

Budget Management

Navigate to Finances → Budget. Set monthly or annual budget targets per expense account. For example, set a R40,000 monthly budget for Rent & Office and R8,000 for Utilities. Once budgets are set, the Expenses Overview dashboard will show a budget vs. actual comparison, highlighting accounts that are over or under budget. This is a powerful tool for controlling costs and identifying overspend early.

Intern Attendance

Navigate to Intern → School Attendance to manage intern school day tracking.

Who Can Submit

Only employees whose account is set to employment type Intern (or whose designation title contains "intern") appear in the intern selection dropdown. Ensure that intern employees are correctly categorised during onboarding.

Log on Behalf of an Intern

Click Log Attendance. Select the intern from the dropdown, enter the start and end dates (business days are auto-calculated), institution name, and optionally the subject/module. Click Submit.

Approve or Reject a Request

Click Approve (with optional remarks) or Reject (with a required rejection reason) on any pending request. Approved days are logged against the intern's attendance record and excluded from absence counts for the period.

Summary Cards

The summary cards at the top of the page show total requests, pending count, and approved count — giving a quick overview of intern school attendance across the organisation.

System Settings & First-time Setup

Follow this checklist when setting up the system for the first time:

1 — Seed Chart of Accounts

Navigate to Expenses → Chart of Accounts and click Seed Default Accounts. This is required before recording any business expenses or generating P&L reports.

2 — Add Departments & Designations

Navigate to Organization and create all departments and job titles before adding employees.

3 — Add Employees

Navigate to Employees → New Employee and create all staff records. Verify banking details carefully — these are used for payroll.

4 — Add Public Holidays

Navigate to Attendance → Holidays and add the current year's public holidays so that leave and attendance calculations are correct.

5 — Add Clients & Vendors

Navigate to Clients and Expenses → Vendors to set up billing contacts and expense suppliers.

6 — Set Opening Balances (if migrating)

If migrating from another system, navigate to Finances → Opening Balance and enter carry-over figures for each account.

7 — Theme & Profile

Click the sun/moon icon in the top navbar to set Light or Dark mode. Click your name → My Profile to update your admin contact details and profile picture.

Logging In & Account Access

Your account is created by your HR administrator. You will receive your login credentials — email address and a temporary password — by email before your first login.

Step 1 — Open the System

Open any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox recommended). Navigate to the system URL provided by your HR department. Bookmark the page for quick access in future.

Step 2 — Enter Your Credentials

Type your email address (exactly as it was provided to you) in the email field. Enter your password in the password field — passwords are case-sensitive, so check that Caps Lock is off. Click Sign In.

Step 3 — First Login

On your first login, you may be prompted to change your temporary password. Choose a new password that is at least 8 characters long and includes a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. Do not share your password with anyone.

Step 4 — Dashboard

After successful login you will be taken to your personal Dashboard, which shows a summary of your payslips, leave balance, pending notifications, and recent activity. The dashboard updates automatically whenever new information is available.

Forgot Your Password?

On the login screen, click Forgot Password?. Enter your registered email address and click Send Reset Link. Check your inbox — you will receive an email with a secure reset link within a few minutes. Click the link and follow the prompts to set a new password. If the email does not arrive, check your spam or junk folder. The reset link expires after a short period, so use it promptly. If you still cannot log in, contact your HR administrator.

Logging Out

Always log out when you are finished, especially on shared or public computers. Click the Logout link at the bottom of the left sidebar, or click your name in the top-right corner and select Sign Out. Your session will end and you will be returned to the login page.

Security: Never share your password with anyone — including IT staff, your manager, or HR. The system will never ask for your password via email or phone. If you suspect your account has been accessed by someone else, change your password immediately and notify HR.

My Profile

Your profile stores your personal and contact information. Keeping it accurate ensures payslips, notifications, and HR communications reach you correctly.

View & Edit Your Profile

Click your name or avatar in the top-right navbar and select My Profile. Your profile page shows your personal details, contact information, and role. Fields you can edit include: first name, last name, phone number, and profile picture. Click Save Changes after making updates.

Change Your Password

On the My Profile page, find the Change Password section. Enter your current password to verify your identity, then type your new password and confirm it. Click Update Password. You will be required to log in again with your new password after saving. Choose a password that is at least 8 characters, uses a mix of upper and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.

Upload a Profile Picture

On your profile page, click the camera icon or Change Photo on your avatar. Select a JPG or PNG image from your device (under 2 MB). Your picture is saved and displayed in the navbar and your employee profile. A professional headshot is recommended.

View Your Employee Details

Navigate to My Details in the sidebar. This page shows your job information managed by HR: department, designation, employment type, start date, salary, and banking details. If any information is incorrect — particularly your bank account number — contact your HR administrator immediately, as errors here affect payroll payments.

Managing Your Documents

You can securely store employment-related documents in the system. These are private to you and authorised HR staff — no other employees can access your documents.

View Your Documents

Navigate to My Details in the sidebar, then click the Documents tab. The list shows all documents stored on your profile, including the document type, description, upload date, and file name.

Upload a Document

Click Upload Document. A form appears with the following fields:

  • Document Type — select the most appropriate type: ID Document, Employment Contract, Payslip, Tax Certificate, Qualification Certificate, Bank Statement, or Other
  • Description — optional but helpful (e.g. "SARS Tax Certificate 2024" or "Matric Certificate")
  • File — click Choose File and select from your device. Supported formats: PDF, JPG, PNG, DOCX. Maximum size: 10 MB per file

Click Upload. The document is immediately stored and visible in your documents list.

Download a Document

Find the document in your list and click the Download icon (↓ arrow). The file downloads directly to your device. This is useful for retrieving your signed employment contract, latest payslip, or uploaded certificates when you need them for a loan application, visa, or job reference.

Delete a Document

Click the Delete icon (trash can) next to the document. A confirmation prompt will appear — click Confirm to permanently delete it. Once deleted, the document cannot be recovered. Only delete files you are certain you no longer need.

Only upload documents that are relevant to your employment record. Do not upload personal files unrelated to work. Do not upload other people's personal documents — this may constitute a privacy violation.

Step 1 — Open Payroll / Payslips

Click Payroll in the left sidebar. The payslips list shows all your payslips from most recent to oldest. Each row displays the pay period, gross salary, total deductions, and net salary at a glance.

Step 2 — View a Payslip

Click on any payslip row or the View button to open the full payslip. The payslip detail includes:

  • Employee Information: your name, ID number, employee number, department, and designation
  • Pay Period: the month and year the payslip covers
  • Earnings: basic salary plus any allowances (housing, transport, overtime, bonuses)
  • PAYE (Pay As You Earn): income tax deducted by your employer on your behalf and paid directly to SARS. This is calculated using the official SARS tax tables for the current year
  • UIF (Unemployment Insurance Fund): 1% of your gross salary, deducted from your pay and matched by your employer. Provides income protection if you become unemployed
  • Pension / Provident Fund: retirement fund contributions, if applicable under your employment contract

  • Net Salary: the final amount deposited into your bank account after all deductions

Click Download PDF to save your payslip to your device. The PDF is an official document suitable for use as proof of income when applying for home loans, vehicle finance, rental agreements, visa applications, or SARS submissions. Always keep a copy of your payslips, especially the annual ones.

If you notice an error on your payslip — incorrect salary, missing allowance, wrong deductions — contact your HR or payroll administrator as soon as possible. Do not delay to the next pay cycle, as corrections may affect tax submissions. Do not alter the payslip document yourself.

Click Leave in the left sidebar. The page shows all your previous and current leave requests with their statuses. Your available leave balance per type is shown as summary cards at the top of the page.

Step 2 — New Request

Click New Leave Request. Complete the form:

  • Leave Type: select the appropriate type —
    • Annual Leave — planned vacation or personal days
    • Sick Leave — illness or medical appointments. A doctor's note is required for more than 2 consecutive days

Cancel a Pending Request

If you submitted a leave request in error or your plans have changed, you can cancel it while it is still Pending. Find the request in your leave list and click Cancel. Once a request has been Approved, cancellation must be done by your HR administrator — contact them directly.

Submit in advance: Annual leave should be requested at least 24–48 hours in advance (or per your company policy). Sick leave should be logged on the same day you are absent. Emergency leave taken without a prior request must still be entered into the system on your return.

Checking Your Leave Balance

Your leave balance shows how many days of each leave type you have available for the current leave cycle.

Where to Find It

Navigate to Leave in the sidebar. Summary cards at the top of the page show your remaining balance for each leave type. The balance updates immediately when a leave request is approved.

Understanding Your Entitlements
  • Annual Leave: typically 15 working days per year under the BCEA, accrued monthly (approximately 1.25 days per month). Your company policy may be more generous — check your employment contract
  • Sick Leave: 30 days over a 3-year cycle. In the first year you may use 10 days, scaling to the full 30 over the cycle. Unused sick leave does not carry over to the next cycle
  • Family Responsibility Leave: 3 days per year. These do not accumulate if unused
  • Study Leave: as per company policy and subject to approval
If your balance appears incorrect or you believe you are entitled to more days than shown, contact your HR administrator. Do not submit leave requests for days you do not have — this results in unpaid leave deductions from your salary.

Step 1 — Open Expense Claims

Navigate to Expense Claims in the sidebar, or via Expenses → Claims. This page shows all your submitted claims and their current statuses.

Step 2 — Start a New Claim

Click Submit Claim. Complete the claim form with the following details:

  • Claim Date: the date you incurred the expense (not today's date unless the expense happened today)
  • Purpose / Description: a clear explanation of what the expense was for and why it was work-related. Be specific — e.g. "Uber to client meeting at ABC Holdings, Sandton — 15 March" rather than just "Travel"

30-day rule: Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense date (or per your company policy). Late submissions may not be reimbursed. Keep original receipts until you confirm payment has been received in your bank account.
9

Tracking Your Expense Claims

You can monitor the status of all your submitted claims from the Expense Claims page at any time.

PENDING

Your claim has been submitted and is waiting for review by your manager or HR administrator. No action is required from you. You will receive a notification once a decision is made.

APPROVED

Your claim has been reviewed and approved. It is now being processed for payment. Payment is typically made with the next payroll run or as a separate EFT — check with your HR team for the reimbursement schedule.

PAID

Your reimbursement has been processed and the amount has been transferred to your bank account. If you have not received it within 2–3 business days of the paid date, check with your HR or finance department.

REJECTED

Your claim was not approved. Open the claim to read the rejection reason. Common reasons include: missing receipt, expense not covered by company policy, incorrect category, or duplicate claim. Correct the issue and submit a new claim referencing the original claim number in the description.

Viewing Your Attendance

The attendance module records your check-in and check-out times. This data may be captured automatically by an access control system or manually by your administrator.

View Your Records

Navigate to Attendance → My Attendance in the sidebar. The list shows your attendance for each working day: date, check-in time, check-out time, total hours worked, and status (Present / Late / Absent).

Understanding Your Status
  • Present — you checked in and out within the expected working hours
  • Late — you checked in after the designated start time
  • Absent — no check-in was recorded for a working day. If you were on approved leave, the system should reflect this. If you were present but marked absent, report it to HR immediately
Discrepancies

If your attendance record is incorrect (wrong time, wrong date, or an absence when you were actually present), contact your HR administrator or line manager with the correct details. Attendance records affect performance reviews and leave reconciliation — do not ignore errors.

Request an Asset

  • Asset Type / Category — specify what you need (e.g. IT Equipment, Office Furniture, Vehicle)
  • Description — describe your specific requirement (e.g. "Laptop for remote work — software development")
  • Reason / Justification — explain why you need the asset and how it will be used for work

Treat company assets with care — you are responsible for their safekeeping while assigned to you. Report any damage or loss to your manager immediately.

Returning an Asset

When you no longer need an assigned asset (e.g. on return from a project, or when leaving the company), you cannot process the return yourself — contact your HR administrator or line manager to formally record the return in the system. Always hand over assets in person and obtain confirmation.

You are responsible for any company asset assigned to you. Report theft, loss, or damage immediately. Do not lend company assets to other employees or take them off company premises without prior authorisation.

Intern School Attendance

This section applies to interns only. It lets you log days spent at your educational institution (school, college, or university) during your internship period.

Step 1 — Open Intern Attendance

Navigate to Intern → School Attendance in the sidebar. This menu item is only visible if your account is set up with an intern employment type. If you do not see it and you are an intern, contact HR to update your account.

Step 2 — Log a Request

Click Log Attendance and complete the form:

  • Start Date — the first day you will be at school
  • End Date — the last day (weekends are automatically excluded)
  • Number of Days — auto-calculated from the date range
  • Institution Name — the full name of your school or college (e.g. "Cape Peninsula University of Technology")
  • Subject / Module — the specific subject or module you will be attending (e.g. "Database Management — DBM301")

Click Submit. Your request appears as Pending. Your supervisor will approve or reject it. Approved days are recorded as school attendance and excluded from your office attendance absence count. You will be notified of the outcome.

Submit school attendance requests as early as possible — preferably before the school period begins. Keep a copy of your timetable or class schedule in case your supervisor requires proof during the review.

Frequently Asked Questions

I cannot log in — what should I do?

Check that you are using the correct email address and that Caps Lock is off. Click Forgot Password? to reset your password. If the problem continues after resetting, check your spam folder for the reset email. If you still cannot log in, contact your HR administrator — your account may have been locked or deactivated.

My payslip shows incorrect salary or deductions.

Contact your HR administrator or payroll manager as soon as you notice the error. Note the exact discrepancy (e.g. "My basic salary shows R10,000 but my contract states R12,500"). Do not alter the payslip — it is a system-generated document. Corrections must go through the payroll office and may result in a revised payslip being issued.

My leave request was rejected. Can I appeal?

Read the rejection reason shown on the leave request. If you believe it was rejected unfairly, speak directly to your manager or HR administrator. They can reverse the rejection and approve the request if appropriate. Do not resubmit the same request without first discussing it — duplicate submissions may result in further rejections.

How do I change my password?

Click your name in the top-right navbar and select My Profile. Scroll to Change Password, enter your current password, then type and confirm your new password. Click Update Password. You will be logged out and prompted to log in with the new password.

I don't see a menu item I expect.

The sidebar only shows sections your role permits. If you believe you should have access to something (e.g. you are a new team leader and expect to see the leave approval view), contact your system administrator to update your role.

My expense claim was rejected. Can I resubmit?

Yes — read the rejection reason and address the issue, then submit a new claim. Reference the original claim number in your description (e.g. "Resubmission of CLM-20240312-001 — receipt now attached"). The original rejected claim remains in your history for your records.

I uploaded the wrong document. How do I remove it?

Navigate to My Details → Documents. Click the Delete icon next to the incorrect file and confirm. Then upload the correct document. If you accidentally uploaded a sensitive document that should not be there, notify HR immediately so they can confirm it has been removed.

I haven't received my payslip email. What should I do?

First check your spam or junk mail folder — payslip emails are sometimes filtered. If it is not there, log into the system and download your payslip directly from the Payslips page. If the payslip is not yet listed, contact your HR administrator to confirm when payroll was processed.

I lost a company asset assigned to me. What do I do?

Report the loss to your manager and HR administrator immediately. Do not wait to see if it turns up. They will guide you through the asset loss procedure, which may include a police report. Do not attempt to replace the asset yourself without authorisation.

My attendance shows "Absent" but I was at work.

Contact your HR administrator or line manager with the correct attendance details (date, approximate check-in time). They can investigate and correct the record. Unresolved attendance discrepancies may affect your performance review or leave reconciliation, so report them promptly.
