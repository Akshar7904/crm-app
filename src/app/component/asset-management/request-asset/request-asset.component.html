How it works
1
Submit Request

Fill in the form with a clear justification for the asset you need.

2
Manager Review

Your manager will review the request and approve or reject it.

3
Asset Assignment

If approved, IT admin will assign an available asset from inventory.

4
Receive & Acknowledge

You'll receive the asset and confirm receipt.

Tips for a quick approval
  • Be specific about what you need and why
  • Include relevant project or client references
  • Mention any deadlines tied to the request
  • For replacements, note the asset tag of the current item
